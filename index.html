How these guides are written

Each guide starts from a concrete production task (a single portrait that needs a sketch-and-color reveal, a 2,500-word lesson plan, an onboarding SOP, or an automated Claude Code documentation pipeline) and walks through the format, pacing and limits before the tool. Where a specific product's numbers are quoted, they are taken from that product's live documentation at the time of writing. Nothing here is sponsored.
